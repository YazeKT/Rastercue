# Models: guidance, not blanket permission

The model browser separates presentation metadata (common/model-catalog.ts) from engine model IDs/files. Seven built-ins: Standard, Lite, High Fidelity, Remacri, Ultramix Balanced, Ultrasharp and Digital Art. Fourteen custom catalogue entries describe the supplied local custom-model set, but their weights are not in this source repository.

Primary sources are linked per entry: Upscayl, the original custom-model repository, Real-ESRGAN creator documentation and applicable Phhofm model documentation. Examples and avoidance advice marked as Rastercue guidance are practical test suggestions, not creator guarantees. No fabricated maximum image sizes, VRAM limits, text restoration or factual-detail guarantees.

Remacri, Ultramix and Ultrasharp retain conspicuous NON-COMMERCIAL notices. Commercial/redistribution rights for other converted weights may remain unverified. Creator statements about an original model do not prove the identity/licence of every conversion. Do not use unknown-rights models for paid client work without checking their actual terms.

Custom import: choose a folder with matching .bin/.param names. Unrecognised files receive filename/native-scale information and unverified guidance rather than invented claims. The app does not download additional weights. Original repository: https://github.com/upscayl/custom-models.

Favourite models and presets are local preferences. Missing preset/history models block restoration without changing settings or silently replacing the model. No automatic model selection/training/cloud processing is included.
