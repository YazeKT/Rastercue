# Rastercue design contract

Designer-focused local workstation, not a new upscaling engine. Selected solid R-and-pixel symbol is reconstructed in SVG; no generated proof texture. Periwinkle #A5A0FF, graphite #14161B, slate #22252D and soft white #F1F2F6. Local Poppins retained for continuity. Brand assets: resources/brand; deterministic production icons: npm run brand.

Permanent 272px left controls and 240px right tools, flexible central preview. Visible primary action must be measured, not inferred from absent scrollbars. At smaller logical heights use workflow pages; settings always paginate. History, model lists and logs may scroll. Inspection bookmarks paginate two per page.

Before/after stays available with output; lens is secondary. Wheel zoom anchors at pointer, pan and divider gestures are separate. Setting guidance gives practical examples; evidence gaps in model catalogue remain explicit.

Independent read-only review identified hidden examples below610px and bookmark overflow. Workflow pages and bookmark pagination address those findings. Actual target screenshots are in tests/artifacts (local QA artifacts, not release source). Expanded high-DPI, translation and designer acceptance remain release gates.
