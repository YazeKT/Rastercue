# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Critique the work, not the person. Harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information and abusive behaviour are not welcome in Rastercue issues, pull requests or discussions.

Respect creators' licences and authorship. Do not upload private client images, proprietary models or credentials. Keep bug reports focused on reproducible evidence, and disclose security issues privately rather than publicly exposing users.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation to protect contributors. Contact options are available through https://github.com/YazeKT; do not publish sensitive report details in a public issue.
